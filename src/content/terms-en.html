5. CUP POINTS RULES

5.1 During the Competition Period, Participants earn Cup Points in two ways: (a) Deposits: five hundred (500) Cup Points are awarded for every US $100 deposited, calculated proportionally to the amount deposited; and (b) Trades: fifty (50) Cup Points are awarded for each Closed Trade, being a trading position both opened and closed within the Competition Period on the Participant's eligible trading account, irrespective of trade size or volume.

5.2 To illustrate: a Participant depositing US $500 and closing 10 trades would earn 2,500 points from deposits ((500 ÷ 100) × 500) and 500 points from trades (10 × 50), giving a combined total of 3,000 Cup Points.

5.3 A 2× multiplier applies to all Cup Points earned during the Last Week, being the period from 00:00 GST on 13 July 2026 to 23:59 GST on 19 July 2026 inclusive. Points earned prior to the Last Week are unaffected and are not retroactively doubled.
